Suggested short description:

Room Refresh is a Shopify app for furniture and home-decor merchants. Shoppers upload a room photo and generate a preview of a product in that space, directly from a product page. AI-generated previews illustrate appearance and placement. They do not provide measured dimensions, guaranteed scale or a live camera-based AR view. Shoppers should check product measurements before buying. Setup uses a Shopify theme app embed and a subscription approved in Shopify. Plans start at $19 USD/month with a 7-day trial; see the current listing for included usage and additional charges.
