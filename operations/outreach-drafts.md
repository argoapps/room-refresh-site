Why relevant: Its dedicated furniture/home Shopify page is a direct audience match.

Subject: A practical room-preview example for Shopify product pages

Hello Skynify team,

Your furniture and home-brand work makes a product-in-room example relevant to the considered purchases your clients manage.

We make Room Refresh, a Shopify app by Digifuse. It combines a room photograph and a product image into an illustrative furniture or decor preview. We have published the inputs, an example result and a short captioned walkthrough at https://www.roomrefresh.app/examples/, with setup and pricing linked alongside it.
